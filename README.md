## Hi, I’m Ilya 👋

## 👨‍💻 About me

I’m a PhD candidate in the [Computer Vision Group](https://www.vision.rwth-aachen.de/) at RWTH Aachen University, supervised by Prof. Bastian Leibe.

🔭 My research focuses on **3D geometry estimation** and **4D scene reconstruction**.

## 🧩 Projects

- 🏹 **[ARROW](https://github.com/ifradlin/arrow)** — Joint 3D reconstruction and point tracking
- 🌊 **[SurGe](https://github.com/karimknaebel/surge)** — Improved surface geometry in point maps
- 🎯 **[Interactive4D](https://github.com/ifradlin/Interactive4D)** — Interactive segmentation of LiDAR sequences
